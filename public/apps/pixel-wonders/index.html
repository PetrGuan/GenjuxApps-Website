The observation loop

A field guide you complete by hand.

    Observe

    Take in the silhouette, markings, and palette before the reference slips away.

    Rebuild the animal in pixels. Wrong colors are part of learning, never a punishment.

    Open new habitats, learn species facts, and grow a personal cabinet of discoveries.

Designed with care

Quiet play. Clear choices.

Your progress stays yours

Core game progress and preferences live on your device. There are no Pixel Wonders accounts, ads, or behavioral analytics.

Play without pressure

No punitive failure state or timer pressure. Accessibility choices include VoiceOver support, color-vision options, larger targets, and reduced effects.

Buy once, if you choose

The optional Foundation Pack is a non-consumable App Store purchase. Free foundation completion remains unaffected.
