Where information goes,

This inventory is written to make the app’s actual data flows easy to inspect. The full legal explanation is in the Privacy Policy.

Three places information can exist

A

Your iPhone

Progress, attempts, unlocks, preferences, streaks, and local entitlement state.

Stays local
B

Apple services

Game Center identity and achievements; App Store purchase and signed transaction records; optional device backups.

Handled by Apple
C

Genjux

No app account, gameplay database, advertising profile, analytics dashboard, or payment details.

Not collected by us
